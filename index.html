Continuous monitoring of non-ionizing electromagnetic fields

EMSafe brings together IoT devices, cloud services, a mobile app, and a web portal to continuously measure each worker's exposure in industrial plants and the levels in every room at home.

Request a demo

Key features

Personal dosimeter

In foundries and metalworking plants, each worker wears a dosimeter that measures their exposure throughout the shift and triggers a local light and sound alert when the threshold for their position is exceeded.

Fixed home sensor

Measures electromagnetic levels in every room and displays them in the app along with their history. When a risk level is reached, it sends an alert and lets you switch off the smart plug from the app.

OHS manager portal

The occupational health and safety (OHS) manager sets thresholds for each position from the web portal. Every change is delivered to the dosimeter and recorded in an audit trail.

Benefits

Exposure measured on the person

The app shows each worker their accumulated exposure for the shift against the limit for their position.

Evidence for inspections

Alerts and threshold changes are logged and traceable, providing a record for regulatory inspections.

No lost readings

If the connection drops, the dosimeter stores readings locally and syncs them once it reconnects.

Control from the app at home

Check the levels in each room and switch off the smart plug when a risk level is reached.

About EMSafe

About the product


EMSafe is a platform for continuous monitoring of non-ionizing electromagnetic fields that brings together IoT devices, cloud services, a mobile app, and a web portal. In foundries and metalworking plants, each worker wears a personal dosimeter, and the occupational health and safety (OHS) manager handles thresholds and evidence from the web portal. At home, a fixed sensor records the levels in each room, and the app can switch off a smart plug when a risk level is reached.

The problem

The environment is measured, not the person


Today, exposure to non-ionizing electromagnetic fields is usually assessed through one-off measurement campaigns that capture the level in an area at a single point in time. However, the field weakens with distance from the source: two people in the same area can have very different exposures depending on where they work and how long they stay near the equipment. Without continuous, individual records, it is not possible to know each worker's actual exposure or to demonstrate it during an inspection.
